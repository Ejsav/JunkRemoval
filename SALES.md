# Selling the $750 junk removal website

**The offer:** I've already built the system. I adapt it to your business and launch it: $750 flat, $375 to start, $375 at launch. The preview is free.

**The target:** established junk removal companies (real reviews, real trucks, steady work) whose website is weaker than the business.

## 1. Find prospects (20 min, 10 prospects)

Search Google Maps for `junk removal <city>` across 3–5 metro areas. Keep a prospect if:

- They have **30+ Google reviews at 4.5★ or better** (a real business with cash flow)
- And their website has at least one of the weaknesses below

| Weakness | How to spot it in 10 seconds |
|---|---|
| No tap-to-call on mobile | Open it on your phone; the number isn't a button |
| No quote form, or a broken one | Submit a test; nothing happens or it goes nowhere |
| No photos of real jobs | Stock photos or none |
| Reviews not shown on the site | 80 Google reviews, zero on the website |
| Slow or broken on mobile | Takes 5s+ to load, text too small, sideways scroll |
| Looks dated | Template from 2015, clashing colours |

Write down **one specific weakness** per prospect. That sentence is your opener.

## 2. Build the preview (30–45 min each, only for the top 2–3)

Follow `RESKIN.md`. Use their real name, logo, colours, services, towns and 3–6 real Google reviews. Publish to `<slug>.demojunkremoval.com`. Test it on your phone before sending.

## 3. Outreach

Send it by text first (owners live on their phones), with email as backup. Never send a pitch without the link.

**Text / DM**

> Hi {first name}, I'm Eric. I build websites for junk removal companies. Your Google reviews are great ({count} at {rating}★), but your site {weakness}. I rebuilt it to show what it could look like: {preview link}. Take a look on your phone. If you like it, I can launch it for you this week.

>

## 4. The call (10 minutes)

1. **Open with their business, not yours.** "How are most of your jobs coming in right now: Google, referrals, Facebook?"
2. **Find the gap.** "When someone finds you on their phone, what happens next?" Let them say it.
3. **Show the preview on their phone**, live. Tap Call, open the quote form, drag the before/after slider.
4. **Price, plainly.** "$750 total. $375 to start, $375 when it's live. You own it."
5. **Close with a next step.** "If you send me your logo and 10 job photos today, it can be live next week."

## 5. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already have a website." | "You do, and it's costing you jobs: {weakness}. This one is built for how people actually book junk removal: on their phone, fast, with a photo." |
| "I get enough work from referrals." | "Then this makes sure the people who Google you after a referral actually call instead of bouncing." |
| "I can do it on Wix." | "You can. This is already built, tested and tuned for junk removal, and you don't spend your evenings on it." |
| "$750 is a lot." | "It's one or two jobs. If it brings in one extra job a month, it's paid for itself in weeks." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to say yes?" Then follow up on day 3. |
| "Can I see other work?" | "This is the system. You're looking at it with your business in it. After launch you're my case study, which is why I'm doing it at this price." |

## 6. Follow-up cadence

- **Day 0:** text + email with the link
- **Day 2:** "Did you get a chance to open it on your phone?"
- **Day 5:** a screenshot of their hero section: "This could be live by Friday."
- **Day 10:** "Closing out previews this week. Want me to keep yours up?"

Then stop. Move to the next prospect.

## 7. After the first sale

1. Launch it perfectly, then test every button on a real phone with the owner watching.
2. Ask for a Google review and a two-sentence testimonial the day it goes live.
3. Screenshot the before and after of their site. That's your first case study.
4. Raise the price to $1,250 for the next client.
5. Offer month-two upsells: missed-call text-back, a quote dashboard, review requests after each job.
